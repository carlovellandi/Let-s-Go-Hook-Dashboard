Study design

For each source asset, five opening-hook treatments are compared while the underlying source content remains fixed. The hook families are Credential, Numbered / Specific, POV / Direct-address, Contrarian / Pattern-break, and Cold Open / Visual-only.

Decision rule

The primary winner is the treatment with the highest 48-hour 3-second retention. Watch depth, shares, saves, comments, follows, and 7-day views are retained as secondary diagnostics rather than substituting for the primary rule.

Prediction calibration

A human pre-test winner and confidence level are locked before reviewing the comparison. The dashboard then reports overall hit rate and accuracy by confidence bucket.

Operating use

The point is not to test forever. High-confidence calls that repeatedly hold can move faster; lower-confidence or genuinely ambiguous hooks are where the test has more decision value.

Study basis. This working view combines logged trial results with oversight-directed extrapolations for unobserved comparison cells. The same variant structure and 48-hour winner rule are used throughout; individual rounds are not separately labeled by basis.
